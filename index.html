DoE & NSF Funded Doctoral Research

1. AI-Driven Battery Health (SoH) & SoC Estimation

At the University of Alabama (supervised by Dr. Jaber A. Abu Qahouq), my research advances intelligent Battery Management Systems (BMS) and health-aware charge control schemes:

  • Consecutive CC Charging Segments: LSTM-ensembled deep learning frameworks for online SoH estimation in batteries with moderate to high degradation.
  • Impedance & Voltage Relaxation: Partial Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS), multi-view cross-geometry SoH estimators, and ML-based Equivalent Circuit Model (ECM) parameter identification across SoCs and cycling temperatures.
  • Hybrid Observer Design: Gradient-Boosted Extended Kalman Filters (GB-EKF) as well as linear and non-linear observers for real-time state estimation.
Microfabrication, RF & Smart Sensing

2. Harsh-Environment Ceramic Antennas & E-Nose AI

At Washington State University Vancouver (supervised by Dr. Praveen Sekhar), I integrated cleanroom fabrication, additive printing, and machine learning:

  • High-Temperature YSZ Ceramic Antennas: Class 100 cleanroom fabrication (photolithography, sputtering, etching) of microstrip patch antennas on 3-mole Yttria-Stabilized Zirconia (YSZ) substrates and RF T-resonator characterization.
  • Inkjet-Printed Flexible Antennas: Designed and fabricated 7 flexible and rigid UWB, mmWave 5G/6G, and circularly polarized antennas using nanoparticle inkjet printing and LPKF milling.
  • Gas Sensor Drift Compensation: Developed AI models to compensate for long-term drift in gas sensors.
